# IT Security Policy

Effective: January 2026.

## Passwords and accounts

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are rotated every 90 days.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on email, VPN, and code repositories.
- Never share your password or MFA codes with anyone, including IT. IT will never ask for them.

## Phishing and suspicious email

- If you receive a suspicious email, do not click links or open attachments.
- Report it with the "Report phishing" button in the mail client, or forward it to the security team at security@acmetech.example.
- You can also call the IT helpdesk at +91 91234 56780 (24x7) for urgent incidents.

## Devices

- Company laptops are encrypted and managed. Do not disable disk encryption or the endpoint agent.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 4 hours.
- Personal devices may access email only through the approved mobile app.

## Data handling

- Do not paste customer data, credentials, or personal data of employees into public AI tools or chatbots.
- Internal documents stay on company storage; do not forward them to personal email.

## VPN and network

- Use the company VPN for all internal systems when working remotely.
- Public Wi-Fi is allowed only with the VPN connected.
