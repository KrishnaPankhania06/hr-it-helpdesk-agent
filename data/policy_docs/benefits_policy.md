# Benefits Policy

Effective: January 2026.

## Health insurance

- Every employee is covered by group health insurance of Rs 5,00,000 per year from day one.
- Coverage includes the employee plus spouse and up to two children.
- Parents can be added at a subsidised premium during the annual enrolment window in June.

## Learning and development

- Rs 20,000 per year learning budget for courses, books, certifications, and conference tickets.
- Pre-approval from your manager is required for any single item above Rs 5,000.

## Internet allowance

- Rs 1,000 per month internet allowance for employees who work from home at least one day per week, paid with salary.

## Employee assistance

- Free confidential counselling (6 sessions per year) through the employee assistance program.

## Provident fund and gratuity

- PF contributions as per statutory norms. Gratuity applies after 5 years of continuous service.
