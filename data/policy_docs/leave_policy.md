# Leave Policy

Effective: January 2026. Applies to all full-time employees and interns.

## Annual leave

- Every employee gets 24 days of annual (earned) leave per calendar year.
- Leave accrues at 2 days per month of service.
- Up to 6 unused annual leave days carry over into the next calendar year. Anything beyond 6 days lapses on 31 December.
- Annual leave must be approved by your manager at least 3 working days in advance.

## Sick leave

- 12 days of sick leave per year, no carry-over.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Public holidays

- 10 company holidays per year, published each December on the HR portal.

## Leave without pay

- Leave without pay (LWP) may be granted at manager discretion after paid leave is exhausted.
- LWP longer than 30 days requires HR head approval.

## How to apply

Apply for leave in the HR portal under My Time > Leave. The balance shown in the portal is the same balance this helpdesk agent reads from the HR database.
