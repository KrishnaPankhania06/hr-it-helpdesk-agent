# Work From Home Policy

Effective: January 2026.

## Eligibility

- All employees may work from home up to 3 days per week after completing 90 days of service.
- New joiners in their first 90 days work from the office so they can complete onboarding, collect their laptop, and meet their team in person. Exceptions need manager + HR approval.
- Interns may work fully remote if their offer letter says so.

## Equipment

- The company provides one laptop per employee (see the IT asset register).
- A one-time home-office setup reimbursement of up to Rs 15,000 is available for chair, desk, monitor, or headset purchases. Claim it in the finance portal with invoices.

## Availability

- Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST; everyone must be reachable on chat and video during these hours.
- Outside core hours, teams set their own schedules as long as deadlines and client commitments are met.

## Security on WFH

- Always use the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
- Lock your screen when away; do not let family members use the company laptop.
